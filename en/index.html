For theatre, concert and touring lighting crews

Plots, patch and riders for stage lighting.

LampOff brings the lighting plot, patch list, focus notes and rider into a single project. It runs on phones and tablets, with no internet connection or account required.

Plot

Scaled plot and hanging positions

Stage dimensions, trusses, side trusses and towers are defined at true scale. Every fixture sits on the plot with its Ch, DMX address, gel and gobo, and beams and focus areas can be shown on the same drawing.

  • Proscenium, black box, arena and TV / studio templates
  • Thousands of fixture models with modes and channel maps
  • GDTF bodies and plot symbols, Open Fixture Library support

Elevations

Front and side views

Tower and truss heights are edited directly in the front and side views, and side and back light angles are read against the height of the stage.

  • Fixture heights on towers
  • View cube on the plot for switching views
  • Manual dimension values

3D

3D stage with haze

3D is the stage from the plot itself: trusses, curtains, scenery and the audience area are built where they sit on the plot. Beams stop where they hit a curtain, scenery or a performer; haze density, house light and floor set the look before the rehearsal.

  • Colour, gobo, zoom, frost and prism
  • Audience areas from the plot as rows of seats in 3D
  • Performer figure, presentation mode and high-resolution images

Patch and focus

Patch list and focus mode

Universe usage, address conflicts and unaddressed fixtures are tracked in one list, and the DMX map shows all 512 channels of each universe. Focus mode follows the focus call in Ch order, and changed patch and notes are stored with each fixture.

  • Auto patch, or patch straight from the map
  • Conflict and missing-address warnings
  • Patch kept when the model or mode changes

Rider

Riders and export

The rider PDF collects the scaled plot, equipment list and power per phase, truss loads, Ch lists for conventional and moving fixtures, instrument schedule, magic sheet, gel cut list and focus charts for conventionals in one document. Patch data is exported as MVR, grandMA2 XML, USITT ASCII and CSV.

  • Throw distance, beam diameter and approximate lux for every fixture
  • Scaled plots on A4, A3 and A2 with revision info, at a scale that fills the sheet
  • MVR compatible with grandMA3, ETC Eos, Vectorworks and Capture
  • Sample rider PDF

Frequently asked questions

Is the LampOff mobile app free?

Yes. The mobile app is free, with no ads and no account required.

How does joining the beta work?

The Gmail address used on Google Play is sent to lampoff.info@gmail.com. Once the address is on the test list, the app can be installed from Google Play. Details are on the Download page.

Is an internet connection required?

Not after the first visit. The internet is only used to download fixtures from the online library.

Where are projects stored?

Only on the phone or tablet where they are used. A project can be saved as a .loff file for backup or to move it to another phone (File › Save).

Which consoles does it work with?

MVR files are read by grandMA3, ETC Eos (Augment3d), Vectorworks and Capture. Patch XML is available for grandMA2, plus USITT ASCII and CSV for other systems.

Can plots from other programs be opened?

PDF lighting plots can be imported: the symbol key is read and fixtures are placed on the trusses by type. MVR and CSV files are supported as well.

How accurate are photometrics and loads?

Throw distance and beam diameter come from the plot, truss trim and the fixture’s beam angle. Lux uses the luminous flux from the GDTF profile when available, otherwise an estimate for the fixture type marked with *. Truss load adds fixture weights and clamps; cable and safeties are not included, so check the truss capacity against the manufacturer’s table.

Is there an iPhone and iPad version?

Yes. LampOff is free on the App Store; iPhone and iPad open the same project file as Android.

Will there be a desktop version?

Yes. A Windows and macOS version is in preparation and will be announced on this site.

Is there a user guide?

An illustrated guide with examples is available in the app under ⋮ › Help, on the Guide page and as a PDF.
