Plot and hanging positions

Stage, trusses, side trusses and towers are built at true scale. Every fixture sits on the plot with its Ch, DMX address, gel and gobo, and the same plot turns into 3D with one tap.

  • Proscenium, black box, arena and TV studio templates
  • Thousands of fixture models, GDTF bodies and symbols
  • Height editing in the front and side views
Plot in the guide

3D stage with haze

3D is the stage from the plot itself: trusses, drapes, scenery and the audience area sit in the same place. Beams stop where they land; haze, house light and floor give the look before the rehearsal.

  • Colour, gobo, zoom, frost and prism
  • Seating rows, performer figures, 3D scenery models
  • Presentation mode and high-resolution images

Patch, DMX map and Focus

Universe usage, address conflicts and unaddressed fixtures in one list. The DMX map shows all 512 ch of each universe in colour; Focus mode follows the focus call in Ch order.

  • Auto patch, or patch straight from the map
  • Conflict and missing-address warnings
  • Changed patch and notes are stored with each fixture

Riders and export

Scaled plot, equipment and power list, truss loads, Ch lists, instrument schedule, magic sheet and focus charts in one PDF. Patch data exports as MVR, grandMA2 XML, USITT ASCII and CSV.

  • A4, A3, A2 scaled plots; revision on every page
  • Throw distance, beam size, approximate lux
  • MVR for grandMA3, ETC Eos, Vectorworks and Capture

Frequently asked questions

Is the LampOff mobile app free?

Yes. The mobile app is free, with no ads and no account required.

How do I join the beta?

Send the Gmail address you use on Google Play to lampoff.info@gmail.com. Once it is on the test list, the app can be installed from Google Play. Details are on the Download page.

Is an internet connection required?

Not after the first launch. The internet is only used to download fixtures from the online library.

Where are projects stored?

Only on the phone or tablet where they are used. A project can be saved as a .loff file to back it up or move it to another phone (File › Save).

Which consoles does it work with?

MVR files are read by grandMA3, ETC Eos (Augment3d), Vectorworks and Capture. Patch XML is available for grandMA2, plus USITT ASCII and CSV for other systems.

Can plots from other programs be opened?

PDF lighting plots can be imported: the symbol key is read and fixtures are placed on the trusses by type. MVR, CSV and DXF files are supported too; SketchUp, OBJ and AutoCAD drawings are prepared for the phone with the Converter.

How accurate are photometrics and loads?

Throw distance and beam size come from the plot, the truss trim and the fixture’s beam angle. Lux uses the luminous flux from the GDTF profile when there is one, otherwise an estimate for the fixture type, marked with *. Truss load adds fixture weights and clamps; cables and safeties are not included, so check the capacity against the manufacturer’s table.

Is there an iPhone and iPad version?

Yes. LampOff is free on the App Store; iPhone and iPad open the same project file as Android.

Will there be a desktop version?

Yes. A Windows and macOS version is in preparation and will be announced on this site.

Is there a user guide?

An illustrated guide with examples is in the app under ⋮ › Help, on the Guide page and as a PDF.
