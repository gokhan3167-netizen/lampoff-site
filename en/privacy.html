Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

LampOff is an app for preparing stage lighting plots and technical riders. This page explains how the app and this website handle personal data.

Data collected

The app collects no personal data, has no accounts and does not track you. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

Storage

Your plots, fixture library and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet. They are never sent to us or to third parties.

Files and sharing

When you export a PDF, PNG, CSV or project file, it is created on your device and the share sheet opens. Only you decide where it goes. Imported GDTF, OFL, PDF, DXF or 3D model files are processed only on your device.

Internet use

The app works offline. It connects only when you ask it to (for example to download fixtures from the online library) or to check for a new version. No personal data is sent.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no visitor tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Files you drop into the Converter are processed only in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Questions: lampoff.info@gmail.com
