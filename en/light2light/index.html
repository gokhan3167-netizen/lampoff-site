light2light · Coming soon

Move your show from one console to another.

light2light converts a show file programmed on one lighting console into a show another console can open. Patch, cues and presets are not rebuilt by hand; the console changes, the show stays the same.

More consoles (grandMA3 → ETC Eos, Avolites, Hog, ChamSys MagicQ…) will follow one by one.

What comes across

Patch and fixture types

Addresses and universes come across as they are. If a fixture is not in the target console’s library, it is built from the source show’s definition.

Cues and timing

Cue numbers, labels, fade, delay and follow times. Tracking is preserved.

Palettes → presets

Intensity, Position, Color and Beam palettes become presets, and cues stay linked to them.

Groups and layouts

Groups keep their selection order; a group and layout is prepared for each fixture model.

Effects

Effects come across with their speed, size and phase, and stop in the cue where they end.

Ready screens

Sequence, layout and preset windows open on the target console, so you can start right away.

How it works

  1. Pick the show file. Drop the source console’s show backup into the program.
  2. Convert. light2light matches the fixtures and writes the cues and presets in the target console’s format.
  3. Open it on the target console. One command brings it into an empty show; a USB folder for the console is prepared too.

Every conversion is checked

After converting, the DMX values of every cue in the source show are compared one by one with the new show, and a report is written. Tested with real theatre shows.
